RAINKEEPER ASSETS

All game artwork and audio is in assets/custom/ and is reused unchanged from
the previous Rainkeeper assignment version:

night-garden.png - nighttime garden and skyline background
bucket.png       - amber/gold collection bucket
rain.png         - blue raindrop
gold.png         - gold bonus raindrop
hazard.png       - red hazard
heart.png        - life indicator
pixel.png        - one-pixel image used for UI panels and button borders
catch.wav        - normal collection sound
gold.wav         - bonus collection sound
hurt.wav         - lost-life sound

These custom assets were made with AI assistance for this project. They are
not presented as hand-drawn or recorded by the student. No new artwork was
substituted when extending the game for Assignment 3.

The BitmapFont is libGDX's bundled default font. libGDX and the Gradle wrapper
retain their upstream licenses; see https://github.com/libgdx/libgdx and
https://github.com/gradle/gradle for the corresponding projects.
